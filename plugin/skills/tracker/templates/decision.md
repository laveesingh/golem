# Decision: <the thing you decided, as a sentence>

> [!NOTE]
> MADR, bare minimum. Status drives the lifecycle: `Proposed` →
> `Accepted` → `Superseded` or `Deprecated`. Don't skip it — a decision
> without a status is just an opinion.

## Status

Proposed · *YYYY-MM-DD*

## Context

The force that makes this a decision rather than a non-event. What's
broken, constrained, or contested right now? One tight paragraph.

## Decision

The choice, stated as a single active-voice sentence. "We will X" — not
"We should consider X". If you can't commit to a verb, this is a
brainstorm, not a decision; switch templates.

## Consequences

What this buys and what it costs. Both columns, honestly.

- **Gains:** ...
- **Costs:** ...
- **New work this creates:** ...

## Rejected alternatives

Each alternative you considered and rejected, with the reason. The reason
is the load-bearing part — future-you will want to know *why* you didn't
take the obvious other path.

- *<alt A>* — rejected because <why>
- *<alt B>* — rejected because <why>

## Open questions

Things still in play that this decision doesn't close. If there are none,
write "None — decision is complete."
