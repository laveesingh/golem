# Brainstorm: <the question you're actually asking>

Frame the question as a single sentence. If you can't, you're brainstorming
two things at once — split the ticket.

## Context

Two or three sentences of background. Just enough that a reader who hasn't
been in your head can weigh the options below.

## Options

Each option gets a short paragraph: what it is, what it costs, what it
breaks. Then a verdict line. Resist the urge to leave "the best option" as
an exercise — that's the whole point of writing this down.
