Turn bar — compact progress lab

Invariant: single-line `● Working/Worked (dur • ↑ • ⚡ • ↓)` in muted grey, blinking ●/○ green dot during live. Data held constant: Working • 12s • ↑ 8.2K • ⚡ 22K • ↓ 341 • 34% (Worked shown where relevant). Terminal width 68ch.

Decision: How to add a compact progress bar (context % or turn %) to the turn bar without breaking the 1-line, 68ch, muted aesthetic.

Axes: scanability / hierarchy · density & wrapping in long sessions · feedback confidence

Width 68ch · muted · blinking dot live Press 1–5 to highlight

0 Baseline — no bar

control
No bar at all. Keep today’s line exactly. The footer’s context meter remains the only progress.
● Working (12s • ↑ 8.2K • ⚡ 22K • ↓ 341)
⋮ transcript …
○ Thinking (4s • ↓ 128)
Benefit: zero added width, no visual noise.
Cost: no glanceable % on the turn bar.
Risk: user watches footer meter (split attention).

A Inline suffix — after stats

suffix
Glanceable suffix. Bar lives after the closing `)` as muted track; % text stays outside. Does not disturb stats scan.
● Working (12s • ↑ 8.2K • ⚡ 22K • ↓ 341) 34%
● Worked (14s • ↑ 8.2K • ⚡ 22K • ↓ 410) 34%
Benefit: high scanability; stats + bar both visible, no line break.
Cost: longest line (~+12ch); may truncate on narrow 60ch.
Risk: wraps on small panes; needs truncation guard.

B Under-bar — two-line layered

layered
Layered meter. Text stays on line 1; a 3px muted track sits directly below. Preserves 1-line scan, adds full-width gauge.
● Working (12s • ↑ 8.2K • ⚡ 22K • ↓ 341) 34%
● Worked (14s • ↑ 8.2K • ⚡ 22K • ↓ 410) 34%
Benefit: full-width bar without lengthening line 1; calm hierarchy.
Cost: breaks 1-line invariant (2 lines); extra vertical space.
Risk: taller widget pushes prompt; blink + bar compete.

C Prefix — before the dot

scan-first
Scan-first. Bar is the leftmost anchor `[████░░░░]`, then `● Working`. Eye lands on progress before stats.
● Working (12s • ↑ 8.2K • ⚡ 22K • ↓ 341)
● Worked (14s • ↑ 8.2K • ⚡ 22K • ↓ 410)
Benefit: strongest progress affordance; works at narrow widths.
Cost: displaces dot/label from the left edge (habit break).
Risk: feels like a “battery” widget, competes with dot.

D Dense — bar inside stats

dense
Dense inline. Bar replaces the `%` token inside `()` — `12s • ▓▓▓░░░░ • ↓ 341`. Shortest line, bar is part of stats.
● Working (12s • • ↓ 341)
● Worked (14s • • ↓ 410)
Benefit: no extra line, no suffix; stays under 68ch even at 60ch.
Cost: loses explicit `34%` text (must infer).
Risk: bar too small to read at low %.
